The game will have a "party" of adventurers trying to clear ...
Each adventurer will have a "discipline" (wizard, knight, etc)
    Character is an instance of their discipline
Each adventurer will be one of a set of races (elf, dwarf, etc)
    Character implements this
Each adventurer will also have a common set of stats
    Health
    Mana
    (?) Experience (leveling up)
    Strength
    Speed\
As the party faces different monsters (clearing "rooms")
    they will take turns performing actions:
    Attack (swing sword, fireball, throw knife)
    Heal
    Dodge
